It’s also the law
  • In the EU we have to support accessibility — European Accessibility Act
  • The law says what has to be accessible: shops, banking, transport, e-books…
  • It doesn’t say exactly how much — there’s no checklist in the law itself
  • In practice everyone follows WCAG — the same rules we use today

What it means for you

Clients ask for it, QA tests it. How much you support is a decision — the more, the safer.
